Data Exploration (Exploratory Data Analysis - EDA)

Objectives of Data Exploration

Understand the Dataset
Examine the structure, size, columns, and data types.
Get familiar with the available data.


Identify Data Quality Issues
Detect missing values, duplicate records, incorrect data types, and outliers.
Ensure the data is clean and reliable.


Identify the Problem or Business Objective
Understand what question needs to be answered.
Define the goal of the analysis.


Explore the Data
Analyze distributions, relationships, patterns, and trends.
Generate initial insights.


Plan the Next Steps
Decide what preprocessing, feature engineering, visualization, or modeling is required.
Create a roadmap for further analysis.
